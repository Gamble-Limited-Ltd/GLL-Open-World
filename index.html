v2.46 · Premium Land · The Real-Size Earth

The real Earth at 1:1 — crisp, vivid, Capital Rift to the horizon.

New in v2.46: PREMIUM LAND — the buyable farm plots around the world now cost what land should: about ten times more per field (a field that ran $80–$340 now runs roughly $750–$3,400 — a fresh player's $8,000 used to buy fifty fields, now it buys five), while the tenant rent rose about three times to match, so land still pays for itself in about 47 days, the same beat as the machines. Fields you already own keep the price you paid. New in v2.45: MACHINES — fill the rooms and floors you own with money-making machines: pick a floor or room you hold in the building inspector, hit INSTALL MACHINES, and a ghost box follows your cursor on your floor (green where it fits, red where it doesn\’t) — click a spot to set each machine down, from a $2,400 Vending Machine to a $52,000 Server Rack. Every machine pays you every day (a blinking work light says it\’s running), they show in the dollhouse whenever you inspect the building, they list in your Empire panel, they count toward your net worth, and any machine sells back for 60%. It all saves with the game. New in v2.44: PATCHWORK — the open country around every city is now a patchwork of buyable farm parcels, like a satellite view of farm country: rectangles of all shapes and sizes in every shade of green, divided by hedge lines, streaming over every mile of dry land past the suburbs. Zoom out past ~300 m and the whole quilt becomes a land catalog — hover any field for its size and price, click it to buy (or walk up to a FOR SALE signpost at street level). An owned field pays tenant rent every day, its hedge border turns gold, it counts toward your net worth, lists in the Empire panel with one-tap teleport, and sells back any time for 60% — and it all saves with the game. New in v2.43: TITLE DEEDS — every room and floor in the houses and buildings is now yours to buy or rent: click a building, pick a floor (big buildings) or a named room (houses and small shops), and the deal sits right in the panel — buy it outright (yours forever, tenants pay you double rent daily, it counts toward your net worth, sell back any time for 60%) or rent it (a two-day deposit, it pays its rent every day, end the lease whenever you like). Every deed lists in your Empire panel with one-tap travel to its doorstep, your holdings glow gold, and it all saves with the game. New in v2.42: NEIGHBORHOODS — houses and neighborhoods now ring every city: six curved ring roads and sixteen tree-named lanes of houses (lawns, driveways, porches, pitched roofs with dormers and chimneys) fill the green belt from the town edge out to ~2.4 km, dry land only, and every house is a real building — click it and the inspector opens it, rooms and all. The city and its neighborhoods stay standing while you walk them, and the woodland belt picks up where the last yard ends. New in v2.41: FLOOR PLAN — clicking any building now opens it with an inspector panel: big buildings list every floor (pick one and the storeys above lift away, leaving that floor's furnished rooms open to the sky, with the camera gliding level with your pick), while houses and small buildings list their rooms by name — Living Room, Kitchen, Bedroom, Stock Room… — and the room you pick glows on the floor. Leasable towers carry their LEASE SUITE button in the same panel, and ✕ or ESC closes it. New in v2.40: OPEN HOUSE — walk up to any building (on foot, or pan the camera right up to one) and it opens like a dollhouse: the shell slices open at waist height and every floor shows its furnished rooms — floor slabs with stair cores, partitions with doorways, offices, apartments, lobbies and shops. New in v2.39: WORLD FOREST — trees now grow all around the world besides in the cities: beyond every city's woodland belt, the open country carries real 3D forest (clumped stands blending into open savanna), placed with the same dry-land gate as everything else — never in water, never on roads, never on vendor plots. The whole world is the real Earth at TRUE SCALE — 111,320 meters per degree, 40,007 km around the equator — and it now reads the way capitalrift.com actually looks: no haze at any zoom, vivid high-contrast mottled olive grass, teal seas and true teal great lakes meeting an azure sky at a crisp horizon, and distant cities standing as dark masses on the land. New in v2.38: DEEP-BLUE LAKES — the land shield no longer renders under lake water at all: lake-touching land cells build from the lake's own raster on a 6.9 km to 215 m grid, so the water is the only surface there and nothing can bleed green through it at any altitude (this was the last green-in-the-lake mechanism — the giant land triangles carried meters of depth error at far zoom, past any separation the water could ride at; verified pixel-exact from street level to the 23,000 km view). New in v2.37: WHOLE-WORLD ZOOM — the zoom ceiling lifts from 34 km to 23,000 km, so you can zoom from street level all the way out until the entire real Earth (40,075 km wide) fits in one view: green continents, blue oceans and the true-shape great lakes, with city beacons visible from orbit and every flat ground layer drawing in explicit height order so nothing flickers at the new extreme zooms. v2.36: TRUE LAKES — every great lake now uses real 10-meter-resolution outlines (Lake Michigan in its actual shape, Green Bay and the Door Peninsula included), the water renders solid clean blue shore to shore with zero holes (an indexed-geometry bug had silently dropped two-thirds of every lake fragment since the Earth rewrite — the "green in the lake" the whole time), and the camera is unchained: the distance cap between your view and your tycoon is gone, so pan anywhere on Earth with detail streaming wherever you look. v2.35: green pastures and blue lakes — the land is fresh mottled pasture green, every sea and lake is clean open blue, and a woodland belt of trees rings every city in the pastures. v2.34: the great lakes finally render as solid water — a clipping bug had dropped 26% of Lake Michigan's surface and grass showed right through it — and the last fog gradient is gone too: the air stays clear all the way to the edge of the world view at every zoom. v2.33: the continental interior finally RENDERS (the old land builder only drew cells the coastline passed through — whole states were holes) with a concavity patch for bays and estuaries, NaN-guarded world tiles. v2.30: depth-stable ground at true scale (no z-fight stripes under cities, inter-city roads no longer swallowed); v2.29: inter-city roads END at the first street of the city they serve — a real T-junction; v2.27: true scale — distant skylines stand as real metro sprawl on true coastlines, coastal roads become causeways with guard pylons, and older saves migrate. Every city still stands in the world before you get there (v2.26), instant travel stays (search, world map [M], orbit flight), and nothing generates in water.
