The real Earth at 1:1 — crisp, vivid, Capital Rift to the horizon.

New in v2.61 — FACTORY FLOOR: your factory shells open up. Click a finished factory → MACHINES: six new heavy machines — from a $26,000 packaging station to a $160,000 injection molder — plus the six classics; twelve machines can share one floor. The shell fades to a cutaway while you set them down click by click, each machine pays every day (~45-day payback), counts in WORTH, and sells back for 60%.
