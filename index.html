v2.33 · Crisp Rift · The Real-Size Earth

The real Earth at 1:1 — crisp, vivid, Capital Rift to the horizon.

The whole world is the real Earth at TRUE SCALE — 111,320 meters per degree, 40,007 km around the equator — and it now reads the way capitalrift.com actually looks: no haze anywhere in gameplay range, vivid high-contrast mottled olive grass, teal seas meeting an azure sky at a crisp horizon, and distant cities standing as dark masses on the land. New in v2.33: the continental interior finally RENDERS — the old land builder only drew cells the coastline passed through, so everything between the coasts fell through to the ocean plane (whole states were holes); every land cell is now classified and filled, with a concavity patch for bays and estuaries, and 794 world tiles that shipped with NaN geometry (invisible holes in the planet) are NaN-guarded. The pale fog that washed out 60% of every zoomed-out view is gone — a wide soft gradient now lives only past 60 km and melts into the sky with no visible band. v2.30: depth-stable ground at true scale (no z-fight stripes under cities, inter-city roads no longer swallowed); v2.29: inter-city roads END at the first street of the city they serve — a real T-junction; v2.27: true scale — distant skylines stand as real metro sprawl on true coastlines, coastal roads become causeways with guard pylons, and older saves migrate. Every city still stands in the world before you get there (v2.26), instant travel stays (search, world map [M], orbit flight), and nothing generates in water.
