- 2026-11-01: v2.30 "Stable Ground" — fixes two reports on v2.29: "the floor below the cities look very glitchy" and "I can't see the roads connecting the cities". Root cause (one mechanism, both symptoms): every 8° world tile's land shield was a single ShapeGeometry — for continental tiles that is TWO 890 km triangles, and the GPU's perspective interpolation of the logarithmic-depth varying across a triangle that size is only good to a meter or two. The 12 mm city plates and the 9.5 cm road tails render in exact anchor-local frames, so the noisy land "won" whole patches of the depth test: grass bled through city floors in green z-fight stripes, and road tails vanished for 10 km past every town. The tails ALSO carried a v2.29 frame bug found on the way: emitTail pushed WORLD-coordinate vertices into a group translated to the city anchor, displacing every tail ~10.8 million meters from its road (mesh:true, junction math correct, rendered nowhere near the city — and the baked tile span only starts at the 10.5 km cap, beyond the fog, so no road was visible at all). Fixes: (1) tails emit ANCHOR-LOCAL vertices matching their group frame; (2) the land of every tile is a 16×16 grid of cell polygons (55 km cells) — measured depth error drops from ~1–2 m to ~3–5 cm, which the 9.5 cm roads clear everywhere; (3) every city gets a FINE PAD — ±12 km of 1.5 km land cells at exact y=0 in the city-anchor frame — and the coarse cells under a pad step down to −10 cm, so the 12 mm plate, the 3–4 cm streets and the road junctions ride an exact surface with nothing able to poke through; (4) lake surfaces grid-clipped the same way and lifted to .075, beaches to .07–.083, lake rims to .09 (all clear of the residual grid error, all still under the 9.5 cm road); (5) grass tint patches no longer spawn under city plates (their 2.5 mm separation was a z-fight even on exact frames).
