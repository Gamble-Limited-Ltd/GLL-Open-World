Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: 2026-11-08

The short version

Open World runs entirely in your browser. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no tracking cookies, no advertising and no payments — and no game server that could collect anything about you. This page says exactly what the game does and does not do.

What stays on your device

Everything about your world — your tycoon's name, balance, plots, carts, buildings, mines and machines — is saved in your browser's local storage under the key openworld-v2. It never leaves your device, and we keep no copy: nothing is uploaded, so nothing of yours can leak or be requested from us. Clearing your browser data, using a private window, or switching to another browser, device or site address simply starts a fresh world — that is the trade the game makes for having no accounts.

What leaves your browser

The game itself makes only these network calls:

  • City search and street data — OpenStreetMap's Nominatim (search) and Overpass (map data) services receive your search terms or the map area you visit, and your IP address, as with any web request. They are governed by OpenStreetMap's own privacy policy. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
  • The three.js 3D library — served from this site together with the game (no third-party request).
  • The update check — on boot the game fetches version.json from this same site to see if a newer build is live. That request stays between your browser and this site.

Hosting logs

Like every website, this site's static host keeps the standard short-lived request logs any web server keeps (page, time, browser, IP). The game adds nothing to that.

No payments, no personal data

The game is play money only — there are no purchases, so there is no payment data. And because the game collects no personal data at all, none is collected from children.

Changes to this policy

If it ever needs to change, the new version is published right here with a new date.
